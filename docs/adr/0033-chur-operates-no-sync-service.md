# ADR-0033: Chur operates no sync service

- **Status:** Superseded by [ADR-0065](0065-peer-only-sync-without-accounts.md)
- **Original date:** 2026-08-27
- **Updated:** 2026-10-20

Chur operates no service. User-operated services and cloud storage are also excluded. There are no accounts or service credentials. Device synchronization is exclusively an explicit direct foreground P2P connection, as defined by ADR-0065.
